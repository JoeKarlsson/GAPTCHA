# Design decisions

Joe's verdicts on GAPTCHA's design. They stand until he changes them.

## 2026-11-24: redesign direction B, "the marker is the page"

Of three mockups (A, a field guide drawn as a technical diagram; B, the marker is the page; C, photos drawn on in red marker with a hidden-marker game), Joe picked B:

- The first screen sits inside the printed marker's thick black frame, and the marker's black triangle is the "Scan a marker" button.
- Huge extra-condensed type (Anybody) with Archivo for text; white, black and one green, taken from the AR arrow in the 2017 scan photo.
- "Find, scan, win" set huge, with the three real photos moving over and behind the words as you scroll (allowed: scroll-driven motion on a concept site; still under reduced motion).
- The hunter feed set as giant rows.
- The favicon and wordmark are the marker itself.
